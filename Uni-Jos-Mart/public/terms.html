Terms and Conditions

Stolen items are not allowed. Anyone found selling stolen items will be handed over to the appropriate authorities, and the account will be permanently removed.

1. Who can use Uni-Jos Mart

Uni-Jos Mart is for students of the University of Jos. Every registered student can both buy and sell. You must give true information, including your matriculation number and school address.

2. Selling rules

You may only sell items you own or have the right to sell. Keep your receipt or other proof of ownership and show it to any buyer who asks. Do not list stolen, counterfeit, illegal or dangerous goods. Mark items as reserved or sold so listings stay accurate.

3. Buying safely

Do not buy randomly from people you are not sure of. Always ask for a receipt or proof of ownership. Inspect the item before paying, meet in a public place on campus, and report anything suspicious to campus security.

4. Our role

Uni-Jos Mart only connects students. We are not a party to any sale and do not guarantee any item. We may remove listings or accounts that break these rules and may share information with the police and university authorities when a crime is suspected.

5. Changes

We may update these terms. Continued use means you accept the update.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Name, surname, optional middle name, profile picture, email, phone number, faculty, department, matriculation number, permanent address, state and local government of origin, and school address.

What others can see

Buyers see only your name, surname, picture, faculty, department, school address and phone number (to contact you about an item). Your matriculation number, permanent address, state and local government of origin and email stay private.

How we use it

To run your account, verify that sellers are real students, and keep the campus community safe. Account, listing, chat and photo data are processed by Appwrite Cloud to provide the service. We do not sell your data. We may disclose it to law enforcement when required or when stolen goods are reported.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time and ask for your account to be deleted.
